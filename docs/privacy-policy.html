PC Architect — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PC Architect is a PC-building app. This page explains what data it collects and why.

Account & Community features

If you create an account to publish builds or browse the community feed, your email address and password are sent to Supabase (our authentication and database provider) to create and verify your account. Passwords are never stored or seen by us in plain text — Supabase handles that. Builds you choose to publish, along with your chosen display name, are stored in our Supabase database and are visible to other users of the app. Favoriting a build records that action against your account.

To remove a published build or delete your account entirely, contact us using the details below.

AI Architect feature

When you use the AI Architect to describe a build in your own words, that text is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a part suggestion. We don't store this text ourselves beyond what's needed to display the result to you. Google's own privacy policy governs how they handle that request.

Data stored only on your device

Builds you create but don't publish, your wishlist, and app preferences (like your chosen accent theme) are stored locally on your device using a local database. This data is never transmitted anywhere and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

What we don't do

PC Architect does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics or trackers, and does not access your device's location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the app's Google Play store page.
