## Short description (80 char max)

## Full description (4000 char max)

Building a PC shouldn't mean twenty browser tabs and guesswork. PC Architect helps you plan a
complete, compatible build — from a bare parts list to a scored, ready-to-buy configuration.

**AI Architect**
Describe what you want in plain language — "a quiet build for video editing under $1500" — and
AI Architect picks real parts from the catalog to match, with a short explanation of why.

**Live compatibility checking**
As you pick parts, PC Architect checks socket compatibility, RAM type, case clearance, and power
draw in real time, flagging issues before you buy anything.

**Build scoring**
Every build gets a multi-axis score — Gaming, Creator, Productivity, Cooling, Noise, and Value —
so you can see at a glance how well-rounded (or specialized) your build is.

**Performance estimates**
Get estimated FPS across a curated set of games at your chosen resolution and settings, plus
bottleneck analysis that explains what's holding your build back and how to fix it.

**Price tracking**
Track price history on individual parts and keep a wishlist of components you're watching.

**3D Workbench**
See your build come together with an interactive, rotatable 3D case view.

**Community**
Publish your builds, browse what others have made, and favorite the ones that inspire you.

Whether you're building your first PC or your fifth, PC Architect keeps every decision grounded
in real compatibility data — no guesswork, no forum-hopping required.
